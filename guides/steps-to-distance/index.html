Steps to distance calculator

10,000 steps is about 6.5 to 8 kilometers (4 to 5 miles) for most adults. The exact distance is your step count multiplied by your step length, and step length depends mostly on your height and your pace.

Distance = steps × step length. If you only know your height, step length ≈ 0.415 × height.

Units

How does the calculator estimate step length from height?

It uses a common rule of thumb: one step is about 0.415 times your height. For someone 180 cm tall, that is about 75 cm, so 10,000 steps come to roughly 7.5 km. A 2025 study that measured the walking of 313 healthy adults found average step lengths from 0.62 m in people 141 to 150 cm tall up to 0.91 m in people 191 to 200 cm tall. The rule of thumb lands close to those values for most heights, and taller people often step a little longer than it predicts.

What is the difference between a step and a stride?

A step goes from one foot to the other. A stride is two steps, from one foot back to the same foot. Phones and pedometers count steps, so this calculator asks for step length, the distance of a single step.

How do you measure your own step length?

  1. Mark a start line on a flat path.
  2. Walk 10 steps at your normal pace and mark where your last step lands.
  3. Measure the distance and divide it by 10.

Why does my phone show a different distance?

Phones and watches use their own models and, outdoors, often GPS. Your step length also changes with pace: research on step counts shows that the number of steps it takes to cover a mile changes between walking and running speeds. A calculator like this one gives a good estimate, not an exact measurement.
